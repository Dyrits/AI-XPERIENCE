A town, one fold at a time.

Lay down roads, add a few homes, and let your little town grow. Every piece is cut from the same sheet of paper.

Connect
Roads must reach the town hall. Buildings need a road on one of their four sides. Roads over water become bridges.
Grow
Cottages house 4 people. Markets provide 12 jobs. Gardens lift happiness. Happy towns attract new residents each day.
Earn
Receive 18 paper daily, plus taxes and 18 per connected paper mill. Construction costs paper; roads cost 3.
Explore
Drag empty ground to move the view. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Inspect buildings to expand them. Erasing refunds half the cost.
Keys
1–7 select pieces. I inspects. E erases. P pauses. Arrow keys select a tile; Enter places. Ctrl / ⌘ Z undoes. Escape cancels.

Your town saves here automatically. Complete the notes for extra paper, or keep building at your own pace.
