Needle

Nº 28

A workbench for regular expressions, and the one tool in this collection you would actually open on purpose. Write a pattern and see what it catches in the text, why it catches it, and what it does when you replace it. The pattern is parsed, not just compiled: every token is coloured, the breakdown names each construct in plain English, and a mistake reports the column it sits in. Capture groups are coloured where they land, the map shows where the matches fall across the text, and a suite of test cases tells you at a glance whether a change broke anything. Runaway patterns are abandoned instead of freezing the tab.
